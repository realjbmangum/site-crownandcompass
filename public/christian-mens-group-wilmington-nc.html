Wilmington, North Carolina

Christian Men's Group in Wilmington, NC

Wilmington runs on tides and seasons. The film crews come and go, the beach fills up all summer and empties by October, and a lot of good men here have friendships that work the same way — plenty of people to grab a beer with on the Riverwalk, almost no one who would notice if they quietly went under. Crown & Compass is built for that second kind of absence.

What it is

A small brotherhood, one book at a time

A Crown & Compass Chapter is a small group of four to eight men who read one book every six weeks and meet to walk it out together. It is Christ-centered and honest. Not a lecture, not a stage, not another thing to perform at. The book gives the group something real to push against, so the conversation gets past the surf report and the work grind into the things men actually carry.

The rhythm is simple and repeats: pick up a book together, read a bit each week and check in along the way, then hold a Gather at week six to close it out before the next one begins. Any man can lead a cycle, and the role rotates, so over time every Brother has taken a turn guiding the room. You can read the whole cadence on the how it works page, and see what we are reading now on the reading list.

How to start or join a Chapter in Wilmington

Here is the straight version, because a faith brotherhood should not start with a stretch. Crown & Compass has one Chapter right now, and it meets online. There is no room downtown near the Cape Fear or out by Wrightsville with your name on the door yet, and we are not going to pretend otherwise.

What that means for you in Wilmington is a real invitation, not a waitlist. Join the online Chapter today and you are reading and meeting with Brothers this cycle, from wherever you are — Midtown, Ogden, Leland across the bridge, or down toward Carolina Beach. And if you want men across a table in your own part of town, this is the good part: you can be the one who starts it. This is a port town; the whole place was built by people who were willing to put out from shore first and figure out the rest on the water. Start a Chapter, invite three or four you already half-trust, and the first local Wilmington gathering happens because you called it.
