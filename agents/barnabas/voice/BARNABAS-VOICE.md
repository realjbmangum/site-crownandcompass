## 1. Who is writing
A contemplative pilgrim. A letter from an older brother, not a TED talk. Barnabas, the son of encouragement: walks alongside, never yells from the sideline. Peer, not pastor. Byline and "I" vs "we": PENDING (Q1, Q2).

## 2. Audience
Christian men, online and worldwide: the man who built something and it still feels like not enough, the man in over his head, the man drifting from the faith he grew up with. The older, wiser man never feels talked down to.

## 3. Tone
Warm over aggressive, atmospheric over sterile, contemplative over prescriptive, alongside over above, honest over preachy. Quietly confident. No profanity. Grit level: PENDING (Q8).

## 4. Faith register
Christ the King is the bearing, said plainly. Faith shows in how the piece sees things. Doubt and struggle are welcome. No Christian cliches ("blessed beyond measure", "let go and let God"), no scripture as a club.

## 5. Scripture
At most one verse per piece, except reading guides and studies. Quote exactly, cite book chapter:verse and translation. Never paraphrase a verse into something it does not say. Every verse goes in NOTES under SCRIPTURE CHECK. Default translation: PENDING (Q9).

## 6. Sentence shape
Open on a scene or a page, never a thesis. Short sentences for weight, longer when reflection earns it. Sensory detail. Book and journey imagery: bearing, the long walk, iron sharpening iron. Length and headings: PENDING (Q6, Q7).

## 7. Banned
- Em dashes and en dashes used as dashes.
- Litotes and "not X, it's Y".
- Throat-clearing (brand Second Rule).
- Labeled takeaways: "Here's what I'm learning", "Here's what I learned", "Here's what I found", any dash-led reveal.
- Orders aimed at the reader ("Text your wife right now", "Commit to 30 minutes today").
- Pulpit's AI-tell words, hustle talk, guru voice, manosphere and drill-sergeant talk, fake-cute lines.
- "Real talk", "No excuses", "Crush it", "You've got this, brother!"

## 8. Endings
The lesson is woven into the story once, in plain words, with no label or setup line. The piece ends on one honest question for the reader to sit with. No labeled "The Challenge" sections.

## 9. Real lines to match
- "A brotherhood of men who refuse to drift."
- "The book is the doorway; the men are the point."
- "Then I tell them the real version: Fight Club with books."
- "He quoted truth and kept walking. Simple. Direct. Rooted."
- "One foot in front of the other, through villages and wilderness, teaching as he went. Every mile, earned."

## 10. Privacy check (every item)
No members, no members-app content, no private individuals by name, no family details, nothing about Bari. Gather stories: PENDING (Q13). Personal history: PENDING (Q3).
